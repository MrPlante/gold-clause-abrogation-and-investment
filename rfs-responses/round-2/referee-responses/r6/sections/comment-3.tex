\bigskip

\noindent The referee is right: the 1933 and 1934 coefficients cannot be statistically distinguished from the 1930 coefficient. We now acknowledge this directly in the paper; Section 5.2 adds:

\begin{quote}
``We acknowledge that, given the width of the confidence intervals around individual year estimates, the 1933 and 1934 coefficients cannot be statistically distinguished from individual pre-treatment estimates such as the 1930 coefficient; the support for the parallel-trends assumption comes instead from the pre-treatment coefficients being individually insignificant.''
\end{quote}

\noindent That pattern of individually insignificant pre-treatment coefficients is what the design relies on.
