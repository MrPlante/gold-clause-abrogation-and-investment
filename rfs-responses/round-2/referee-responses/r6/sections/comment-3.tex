\bigskip

\noindent The referee is right: the 1933 and 1934 coefficients cannot be statistically distinguished from the 1930 coefficient. Rather than argue the point, we now say it in the paper. Section 5.2 adds:

\begin{quote}
``We acknowledge that, given the width of the confidence intervals around individual year estimates in a sample of this size, the 1933 and 1934 coefficients cannot be statistically distinguished from individual pre-treatment estimates such as the 1930 coefficient; the support for the parallel-trends assumption rests on the full pre-treatment pattern.''
\end{quote}

\noindent That pattern---pre-treatment coefficients that are uniformly small and individually insignificant, with the two years immediately preceding the litigation essentially at zero---together with the contrast against the omitted 1932 base year, is what the design relies on.
